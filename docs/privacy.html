Quilt Bee Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Quilt Bee is a quilt block designer and quilt planner. It is built so that your work stays with you.

What Quilt Bee collects

Nothing. Quilt Bee does not collect, store or transmit personal information, usage data, analytics or crash reports. It has no account system and no server of its own.

Where your work lives

Blocks, quilt layouts, fabric colors and any photos of fabric you add are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Photos

If you choose to use a photo of real fabric, the app asks for access to that photo through the standard system picker. The image is stored on your device with your block and is never sent off the device.

Purchases

Quilt Bee Plus is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Quilt Bee never sees your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Quilt Bee does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
