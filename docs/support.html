Quilt Bee Support

Quilt Bee helps you design a quilt block, lay out the whole quilt, and get the list of pieces to cut with the fabric to buy.

How it works

  1. Set up the block. Choose the grid and finished size, or start from a classic block.
  2. Pick a piece. Tap a piece, then tap a square on the block. Tap it again to turn it.
  3. Choose fabrics. Tap Main fabric or Background, then pick a color, a print, or a photo of real fabric. Tap any patch to repaint it.
  4. Lay out the quilt. Rows, columns, sashing, cornerstones, borders, and on point for diamonds.
  5. Cut with confidence. Pieces to cut lists every piece by fabric and how much to buy.

Free and Plus

Baby and lap quilts are free, and so is your first throw. Bed sizes and more throws are in Quilt Bee Plus, a one-time purchase. Bought Plus on another device? Open the Plus panel and tap Restore purchases.

Your work

Everything is saved on your device only. Use Save to keep a block and Open to bring it back.
